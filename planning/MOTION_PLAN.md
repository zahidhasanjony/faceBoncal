## Motion pattern rules used

- Headline: spring opacity + 28px lift + scale, category pill and label delayed 1–6 frames.
- Stage: individual SVG geometry driven by frame-based springs, sine rotations, staggered assembly and timed draw-on strokes; no CSS animation loops.
- Detail card: independent raised spring and subtle border, separated from illustration; no overlap with subtitle card.
- Caption: each of 47 original cues is sequenced by absolute frame bounds; 6-frame faster exit, Bengali line-height 1.46.
- Background: slow grid/cyan light drift; stage and subtitles are visually dominant.
- SFX: scene-selective low-level whoosh, tap, impact, shimmer; supplied narration is the main audio.
- GitHub: font installer downloads 400/500/600/700 weight Hind Siliguri before typesetting starts.
